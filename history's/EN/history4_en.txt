A mother sent her daughter to the store to buy some bread.

The girl bought the bread, but as she was about to leave, a woman in a blue dress asked her:

— Girl, isn’t this your blue bag?

The girl looked at the beautiful blue bag — and liked it so much, she said:

— Yes, it’s mine.

The woman gave her the bag. The girl ran home right away.

— Look, Mom! What a pretty blue bag I got!

But when the mother saw the bag, she grabbed it and threw it out the window.

— Never take a blue bag from anyone ever again, — she warned.

That night, the girl woke up — someone was calling her from outside. She went to the window and saw the woman in the blue dress, holding the blue bag.

— Girl, — the woman said, — this is your bag. Take it.

Then the woman’s arms started to stretch… and reached the girl’s third-floor window. Her hands were blue too.

The girl took the bag anyway and backed away — but the arms kept stretching. They reached into the room, grabbed her, and strangled her.

In the morning, when her mother came in, the girl was dead. Her hands were blue.
