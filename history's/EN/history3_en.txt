There was once a boy who could run so fast, no one could catch him — not even grown-ups. And grown-ups often tried, because the boy constantly teased and mocked people.

One day, he teased an old woman, calling her an “old turtle.”

— You better watch your tongue, — she told him. — It’s too wild. One day it might turn into a snake.

But the boy just laughed and mocked her even more.

— Better to have a snake in my mouth than see an old turtle like you.

That night, the boy had a dream. His tongue began to grow… and then turned into a snake. And instead of a snake’s head, it had the face of the old woman he’d mocked. He tried to run, but he couldn’t. He tried to scream for help, but he couldn’t — his tongue was gone…

When he woke up the next morning, his hair was white as snow. He stopped speaking entirely. All day, he only mooed and made strange noises. No one could understand him.

The next night, the boy went to sleep… and never woke up.

In the morning, they found him dead. On his arms were marks — like snake bites.
